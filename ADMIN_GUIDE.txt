🎯 MANAGING BOOKINGS (WHAT YOU'LL DO MOST)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

When a customer books:
1. You'll see it in "Pending Requests" on your dashboard
2. Click "View" to see all details
3. Click "Approve" if you can do it
4. Click "Reject" if you can't

To chat with customers:
- Click the purple "Chat" button
- Type your message
- They'll see it in their dashboard

To mark a job complete:
- Go to "Bookings" tab
- Find the booking
- Click green "Complete" button

👥 HOW IT WORKS FOR CUSTOMERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NEW CUSTOMERS:
1. Click "Book Now" on your website
2. Choose service (dog walking, pet sitting, etc.)
3. Enter their email, password, name, address, pet details
4. Pick date and time
5. Submit booking
6. Wait for YOU to approve it

RETURNING CUSTOMERS:
1. They log in (no need to enter details again!)
2. Choose service, date, time
3. Submit - done!

They can:
- See all their bookings in "My Dashboard"
- Chat with you about bookings
- Cancel pending bookings

📊 UNDERSTANDING YOUR DASHBOARD
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

DASHBOARD TAB:
- Shows numbers: total bookings, pending, completed, money earned
- Pending Requests: new bookings waiting for you
- Recent Activity: what's been happening

BOOKINGS TAB:
- ALL bookings in one place
- Filter by status (pending, confirmed, etc.)
- Approve, view, chat, or mark complete

CUSTOMERS TAB:
- List of all your customers
- See their pets and booking history
- Click "View Details" to see everything

CALENDAR TAB:
- See upcoming confirmed bookings
- See past completed bookings
